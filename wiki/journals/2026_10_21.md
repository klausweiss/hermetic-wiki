- [[hermetic]] was born since none of the [[alternatives]] I researched served all my needs
  - I used [[emanote]] with a custom theme for some time, but I was missing creating empty pages with no files underneath by just linking to them.
  - At the time of researching [[quartz]] as an alternative, it wasn't a standalone executable. You rather cloned its repository and set up a node environment to work with it, which isn't something I wanted to do in each project I wanted a wiki in.
  - I am a [[logseq]] user, but it is not a good fit for running in-project. Having to switch between graphs in a single app didn't feel natural for a project wiki.
